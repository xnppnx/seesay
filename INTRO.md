# SeeSay 项目简介 / Project Introduction

## 中文

**SeeSay 是一双"会说话的眼睛"。** 按一下按钮，它会拍下眼前的画面，用语音告诉你"看到了什么、写了什么字"——全程不用看屏幕。

它完全离线运行，不需要联网、不需要账号、不上传任何照片，专为视障及低视力人士设计；目前在 CardputerZero 开发机上验证，最终目标是做成随身穿戴的辅助设备（如智能眼镜）。

## English

**SeeSay is a pair of "speaking eyes."** Press one button and it takes a photo of what's in front of you, then tells you out loud what it sees and any text it reads — no screen needed.

It runs completely offline: no internet, no account, and no photos ever leave the device. It is designed for blind and low-vision users, and is currently being validated on a CardputerZero development device, with a wearable assistant (such as smart glasses) as the long-term goal.
